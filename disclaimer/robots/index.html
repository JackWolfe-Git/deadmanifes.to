Purpose

deadmanifesto is the living electronic press kit and portfolio of Ginger Deadman. It presents the discography, photo/press material, career/games work, lifestyle writing, contact routes and copyright/disclaimer info in one ultra-minimalist brutalist site. Finished pages are public; unfinished sections are unlinked and marked noindex until they are ready.

Public structure

  • index.html — Splash
  • music/index.html — Discography, live Spotify profile and cover slots
  • ginger-deadman/index.html — Artist profile
  • ginger-deadman/about/index.html — About
  • ginger-deadman/about/epk-bio/index.html — EPK bio
  • deadmanifesto/index.html — Brand hub
  • deadmanifesto/manifesto/index.html — Manifesto
  • deadmanifesto/games/index.html — Steam profile and link to credits
  • deadmanifesto/career/index.html — Selected game credits and professional history
  • contact/index.html — Contact and socials
  • disclaimer/index.html — Disclaimer
  • disclaimer/changelog/index.html — Changelog
  • disclaimer/robots/index.html — This page

Held back and unlinked: services, editorial slots, gallery and lyric pages. They are marked noindex until they are ready.

Games carries the live Steam profile only; other platform cards stay off the public surface until URLs exist. Music carries verified release titles and the live Spotify profile; release metadata and additional streaming links are author-supplied.

Site files

  • 404.html — Not-found page
  • robots.txt — Crawl policy and sitemap pointer
  • sitemap.xml — Public URL index

Assets & data

  • style.css — single shared stylesheet, dark/light/orange mode rules, mobile breakpoints, image slots and release grid
  • assets/theme.js — theme mode persistence + notice
  • assets/images/ — SVG icons, image slots and the live favicon; local album covers and press images to follow
  • content.md — editorial map, asset ledger and hold list (not a copy source)
  • PLAN.md — launch-prep work queue and decisions

Maintenance rule

Any structural change — a new page, removed page, moved section, new global asset or persistent setting — is reflected here immediately, with a matching entry in /disclaimer/changelog/. Public structure and the held-back list are updated together. Public pages carry a canonical URL, description, Open Graph/Twitter tags and a page-level H1. The changelog uses 0.1.x during launch prep; the image layer is 0.2.0; public launch is 1.0.0.
