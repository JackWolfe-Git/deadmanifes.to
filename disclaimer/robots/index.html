Purpose

deadmanifesto is the living electronic press kit and portfolio of Ginger Deadman. It presents the discography, photo/press material, career/games work, lifestyle writing, contact routes and copyright/disclaimer info in one ultra-minimalist brutalist site. Finished pages are public; unfinished sections are unlinked and marked noindex until they are ready.

Public structure

  • index.html — Splash
  • music/index.html — Discography, Spotify profile link and cover slots
  • music/albums/*/index.html — 6 public release templates, linked from Listen and noindex until credits and remaining metadata arrive
  • music/albums/flips/*/index.html — 27 Flips track templates, linked from the Flips page and noindex until lyrics and credits arrive
  • ginger-deadman/index.html — Artist profile
  • ginger-deadman/about/index.html — About and identity
  • ginger-deadman/about/epk-bio/index.html — Print-friendly EPK bio
  • deadmanifesto/index.html — Brand hub
  • deadmanifesto/manifesto/index.html — Manifesto and AI credits
  • deadmanifesto/games/index.html — Steam profile, game covers, platform cards and selected credits
  • deadmanifesto/career/index.html — Game credits and professional history
  • contact/index.html — Contact links, LinkedIn, RSS, channel and email placeholders
  • disclaimer/index.html — Disclaimer
  • disclaimer/privacy/index.html — Privacy notice
  • disclaimer/changelog/index.html — Changelog
  • disclaimer/robots/index.html — This page
  • search/index.html — Local search results page

Held back and unlinked: services, editorial slots, gallery and lyric pages. They are marked noindex and show a coming-soon panel on direct visits; they are not linked from the public menu.

Six further release and collaboration templates are also held back and unlinked. They are marked noindex and kept for future plans.

Games carries the Steam profile link, the four verified game covers and credits, Xbox and PlayStation username cards. Music carries the six public release titles, the 27-track Flips (Remix Collection) tracklist from 2021 with named noindex track pages, and a Spotify profile link; release metadata, tracklists and streaming links are author-supplied. The six public album templates are excluded from search, RSS and the sitemap until they are filled.

Site files

  • 404.html — Not-found page
  • robots.txt — Crawl policy and sitemap pointer
  • sitemap.xml — Public URL index
  • feed.xml — Changelog RSS feed
  • search-index.json — Static local search index

Assets & data

  • style.css — single shared stylesheet, dark/light/orange mode rules, mobile breakpoints, image slots, album/game cards, search, footer privacy link and print styles
  • assets/theme.js — theme mode persistence + notice
  • assets/site-ui.js — header search control, dynamic age and print button
  • assets/search.js — local search results renderer
  • assets/fonts/ — self-hosted Fragment Mono and its OFL licence
  • assets/images/ — local SVG icons, image slots, game covers, the 404 WIP image and the favicon; local album covers and press images to follow
  • content.md — editorial map, asset ledger and hold list (not a copy source)
  • PLAN.md — launch-prep work queue and decisions

No analytics, cookies, tracking pixels or third-party embeds. Browser storage is limited to the theme preference. GitHub Pages hosting logs are disclosed on the privacy page.

Maintenance rule

Any structural change — a new page, removed page, moved section, new global asset or persistent setting — is reflected here immediately, with a matching entry in /disclaimer/changelog/. Public structure and the held-back list are updated together. Public pages carry a canonical URL, description, Open Graph/Twitter tags and a page-level H1. The changelog uses 0.1.x during launch prep: 0.1.6 is this content/privacy/search/template batch, 0.1.7 is QA and deployment, the image layer is 0.2.0, and public launch is 1.0.0.
