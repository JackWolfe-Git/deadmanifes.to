EPK Bio

Artist Biography

Ginger Deadman is the artist behind deadmanifesto, working across experimental hip-hop, electronic music, visual work and games.

The public deadmanifesto catalog includes Obsolescence, Obliviability, The Unreal Tapes and the Flips series.

Alongside the music, Ginger Deadman has worked in game QA and production on titles including Helldivers 2, LEGO Horizon Adventures, Ravenbound and Call of the Wild: theAngler.
