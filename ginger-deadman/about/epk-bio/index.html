EPK Bio

Artist Biography

Ginger Deadman is an alias of Jack Wolfe, the artist behind deadmanifesto. They are a multimodal artist, working across experimental hip-hop, electronic music, visual work and games.

The deadmanifesto catalog includes an archive of lyrics and mechanical productions from Ginger Deadman, functioning as an open distribution and streaming platform built on freeware with as much transparency as possible.

Jack Wolfe works professionally as a QA Tester; all professional inquiries must relate to the Ginger Deadman / deadmanifesto projects.
