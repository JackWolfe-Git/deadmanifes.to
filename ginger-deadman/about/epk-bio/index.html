EPK Bio

Artist Biography

Ginger Deadman is the artist behind deadmanifesto, working across experimental hip-hop, electronic music, visual work and games.

The deadmanifesto catalog includes Orange, Obstinancy, Obliquity, The Obsolescence Tapes, Obliviability, The Unreal Tapes, the Flips series, and collaborations Metro House, Cultus Ex Cultura and Unapology.

Alongside the music, Ginger Deadman has worked in game QA and production on titles including Helldivers 2, LEGO Horizon Adventures, Ravenbound and Call of the Wild: The Angler.
