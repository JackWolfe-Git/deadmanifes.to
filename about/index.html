ALL OF THIS IS MINE

The work, audio, visuals and words published across this website and my connected projects, along with every view and opinion expressed here, are solely my own and do not represent my employer.

Any memes or external media I share are probably there because I found them funny, interesting, or because there was some ironic intent behind them. Where appropriate, I’ll credit the original work.

My lyricism and poetry are open to interpretation. Most of it is abstract, or inspired by a billion who picked up a pen before me. Not everything is written from my own perspective, and some of it will touch on dark themes.

Any use of AI in my original content will be clearly labelled. That said, third-party media and memes may not be disclosed properly by whoever originally uploaded them. Don't believe everything you see on the internet. The models used to build this site are listed in the AI credits.

Any sponsored endorsement will be disclosed, although I don't intend on taking any sponsorships any time soon.

Please challenge me on my beliefs. I'm just out here moving through life and trying to live with some degree of empathy. If you disagree with something I say, make or share, feel free to reach out. I'm always happy to have a discussion, and maybe you'll change my mind.

I thought I'd take this opportunity to say something else, too.

I don't want fame, money or notoriety.

I just want to be heard.

To me, this space and my online presence are creative outlets. Somewhere for me to express myself artistically, politically, and generally just make sense of whatever is going on inside my head.

I've been putting things on the internet for as long as I can remember, and it's important to me that I can continue to express myself freely.

I'm a zillennial multimodal artist working in the Games industry as a Senior QA. I've been a songwriter, producer, session musician, bartender, hotel night porter, Sandwich Artist, graphic designer, expeditor, actor, bicycle courier, developer, teacher, critic, video editor, photographer, roadie, warehouse worker, poet and skater.

The list of things I've done, made, been interested in and occasionally convinced myself I could make a living from is extensive.

I'm an eclectic observer. I enjoy pretty much every form of media in pretty much every genre, and my criticism is usually more interested in artistic intention than whether the final product is technically 'good'.

You can call me Jack, or Ginger.

My artist name is Ginger Deadman, and my chosen name is Jack Aodh Ginger Kilbride Wolfe.

Purpose

deadmanifesto is the living artist archive and portfolio of Ginger Deadman. It presents the discography, biography and visual material, career and game credits, lifestyle writing, contact routes and copyright/disclaimer info in one ultra-minimalist brutalist site. Finished pages are public; unfinished sections are unlinked and marked noindex until they are ready.

Public structure

  • index.html — Splash
  • music/index.html — Discography, Spotify profile link and cover slots
  • music/albums/*/index.html ? 9 release pages, linked from Listen and noindex until credits, lyrics and streaming links are complete
  • music/albums/*/*/index.html ? 99 track pages, linked from their releases and noindex until lyrics and credits arrive
  • ginger-deadman/index.html — Artist profile
  • ginger-deadman/about/index.html — About and identity
  • ginger-deadman/about/bio/index.html — Artist biography
  • deadmanifesto/index.html — Brand hub
  • deadmanifesto/manifesto/index.html — Manifesto and AI credits
  • deadmanifesto/career/index.html — Game credits, cover art and professional history
  • contact/index.html — Contact links, LinkedIn, Steam, Xbox, PlayStation, EA, RSS and email placeholder
  • about/index.html — About, disclaimer, changelog and robots
  • about/tools/index.html — LLMs, workspaces, AI-assisted, hardware and traditional tools
  • about/privacy/index.html — Privacy notice
  • about/index.html#changelog — Changelog
  • about/index.html#robots — This page
  • search/index.html — Local search results page

Held back and unlinked from the main menu: editorial, service and gallery templates. Lyric and track pages are linked from their albums but remain noindex while lyrics, credits and remaining metadata are completed. Held-back project templates are omitted from the human sitemap.

Six further release and collaboration templates are also held back and unlinked. They are marked noindex, kept for future plans and omitted from the human sitemap.

Career carries the four verified game covers and credits, six redacted placeholders and the full professional history. Contact carries the Steam profile link and Xbox, PlayStation and EA username cards. Music carries the Tapes and Flips releases, their track pages and a Spotify profile link: oxox, mainframe, Denial, Unreal, Obliviability, Obsolescence, Club Foot, Flips and Flips 2. Catbox streams are live on Flips and Obsolescence; the newer tapes currently link to SoundCloud. Release and track pages remain excluded from search, RSS and sitemap.xml until their metadata is complete.

Assets & data

  • style.css — single shared stylesheet, dark/light/orange mode rules, mobile breakpoints, image slots, album/game cards, search, footer links and print styles
  • assets/theme.js — theme mode persistence + notice
  • assets/site-ui.js — header search control, dynamic age and print button

  • assets/fonts/ — self-hosted Fragment Mono and its OFL licence
  • assets/images/ — local SVG icons, image slots, game covers, the 404 WIP image and the favicon; local album covers; artist portrait to follow

No analytics, cookies, tracking pixels or third-party embeds. Browser storage is limited to the theme preference. GitHub Pages hosting logs are disclosed on the privacy page.

Maintenance rule

Any structural change — a new page, removed page, moved section, new global asset or persistent setting — is reflected here immediately, with a matching entry in /about/#changelog. Public structure and the held-back list are updated together. Public pages carry a canonical URL, description, Open Graph/Twitter tags and a page-level H1. The 0.1.x line covered launch preparation; the current interaction pass is 0.2.x, and public launch is 1.0.0.
