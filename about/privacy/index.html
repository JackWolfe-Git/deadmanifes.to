Privacy

The short version: no analytics, no advertising, no cookies and no third-party embeds. This page explains the small amount of data the site does use.

Last updated: 8 November 2026.

What this site stores

deadmanifesto stores one preference in your browser using localStorage:

  • dm-mode — your chosen colour mode (dark, light or orange).

This value stays in your browser. It is not sent to me or to anyone else. You can remove it at any time by clearing site data in your browser settings.

There is no cookie consent banner because the site does not use non-essential cookies or trackers.

Hosting

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, including IP address, browser user-agent, requested URL and time of request, for security and operational purposes. I do not receive or analyse those logs as analytics. GitHub's own privacy statement applies to its hosting service.

Search

Search runs in your browser against a local index. There is no external search service and I do not log searches. The query appears in the page URL, so it may appear in GitHub Pages hosting logs in the same way as any other requested URL.

RSS

The RSS feed is a static file. Your feed reader requests it like any other page. I do not add tracking pixels or unique identifiers to the feed.

External links

Links to Spotify, Steam, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, RateYourMusic, LinkedIn, Xbox and other sites open only when you click them. Those sites have their own privacy policies and may collect data once you arrive. This site does not load their players, fonts, icons or widgets.

Email

Direct email is coming soon. Until then, the contact page lists the public social routes. I do not run a mailing list or automated tracking on messages.

Your choices

  • Clear dm-mode through your browser's site-data controls.
  • Use the site without JavaScript; the pages remain readable, but search will not run. The privacy link remains in the footer.
  • Contact me through the public routes on the contact page if you have a question about this notice.

Changes

If this notice changes, the updated date above and the changelog will record it.
